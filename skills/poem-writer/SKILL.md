# Poem Writer

## What This Skill Does
writes a personalised, heartfelt poem for any occasion and delivers it as a beautifully formatted PDF or Word document ready to print, frame, or share.

## When to Use
- When you want to express deep emotions that words alone can't capture

- For memorial or tribute poems, or poems marking important transitions

- The occasion or reason (anniversary, birthday, graduation, farewell, celebration, loss, or just because)
